When an independent journalist discovers unethical practices happening under the watch of a "reverse-energy drink" company and its CEO, they must find a way to expose the truth while also not succumbing to the repeated efforts of the company trying to silence them.

# Z-FUEL: OUT COLD
Z-FUEL: OUT COLD is a winter themed flavor for the [[obsidian-paulhacknotes/Mimikked/Shows/Z-FUEL/Story/Z-FUEL|Z-FUEL]] product line. Its debut features a commercial in which [[Waylon Warner]] sings a parody version of "God Rest Ye Merry Gentlemen."
## Endurance Test
The Endurance Test is a human subject trial conducted by [[Zeta Site]] during the fourth iteration of the [[De-Energizing Compound]]. The trial measures the onset and progression of the compound's effects, specifically how long it takes for subjects to begin exhibiting signs of de-energization following administration.

# NOTES
- [[Ethan Brooke]] is the key to being able to stop [[Zeta Site]] and [[Waylon Warner]].
- Throughout the story [[Waylon Warner]] changes from a passive force to an active force against the protagonist.
- [[Waylon Warner]]'s main weapon is the [[De-Energizing Compound]] installed into remote controlled gas masks.
- The mascot for [[obsidian-paulhacknotes/Mimikked/Shows/Z-FUEL/Story/Z-FUEL|Z-FUEL]] is a zombified caricature of [[Waylon Warner]]. At the end of the story Waylon uses an experimental DE-6 compound and as a result resembles the mascot.
- Ethan Brooke is eventually captured by [[Zeta Site]] and the investigators devise a plan to rescue him by going undercover. The undercover investigator sacrifices themselves in order to rescue Ethan.
- [[Zeta Site]] has a partnership with Cirrostore, the company that produces CHNO Boost Patch, which eventually gets dissolved due to concerns of public safety.
- [[Waylon Warner]] suffers from an illness that pushes his research for DE-6 eventually turning into the [[obsidian-paulhacknotes/Mimikked/Shows/Z-FUEL/Story/Z-FUEL|Z-FUEL]] mascot.
